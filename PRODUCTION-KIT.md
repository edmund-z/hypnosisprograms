# Production Kit — Recording Days

Version 1.0 · 2026-07-08 · companion to PROGRAM-STRUCTURE-SPEC.md v2.0 and
LISTENING-MAPS.md v1.0

Everything needed to walk into a room and batch-produce all four programs:
**36 audio tracks (~5.5 h finished) + 13 videos (~28 min)** in one video day
and two audio days, plus one mix pass.

Boundary: this kit gives each track's *architecture* — its job, beats, and
timestamps. The words are yours. Scripts written in your voice, from these
beat sheets, are what make it face-first instead of stock.

---

| Day | Output | Notes |

all 36 tracks and cannot change after day A.

## 2. Audio technical spec

Hand this section to any engineer; there should be zero questions.

**Recording**

- 48 kHz / 24-bit WAV, mono voice channel.
- Large-diaphragm condenser or broadcast dynamic (SM7B-class), 10–15 cm,
  slight off-axis, pop filter. **Mark the mic position with tape** — the
  same voice-to-mic geometry across both days is what makes 36 tracks sound
  like one product.
- Treated quiet room. Noise floor under −60 dBFS. Phones out, HVAC off.
- Record everything with 2 s of room tone at head and tail; capture 60 s of
  clean room tone once per day.
- No processing while tracking. Raw in, decisions in post.

**Mastering targets**

| Track class | Integrated loudness | True peak | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sittings, Encores, Standbys | −16 LUFS | −1.5 dBTP | Podcast/mobile standard; survives phone speakers and AirPods alike. |
| Sleep-facing tracks (The Night Before, The Wind-Down, After Study — Sleep) | −18 LUFS | −1.5 dBTP | Quieter class so a bedtime listener never touches the volume. |

- Gentle compression only (2:1, slow), de-ess, de-click, breath-level — do
  not remove breaths. Breath is pacing.
- Music/ambience beds sit at −28 to −32 LUFS under voice, one shared
  palette across all four programs, ducked 2–3 dB under speech, never
  competing during suggestion passages. Beds fade fully out under the final
  minute of sleep-facing tracks.

**Delivery formats**

- Archive master: 48/24 WAV, voice-only + full-mix, per track.
- Distribution: MP3 320 kbps CBR 44.1 kHz, tagged per §5. (One format;
  every target — room, feed, download zip — uses the same file.)

## 3. Voice, pacing, and the anchor

- **Sittings:** open near your speaking pace (~130 wpm), settle to ~90 wpm
  by the deepener, slower with longer engineered pauses in change-work
  passages. Silence is content: a 15:00 Sitting should carry roughly 2–3
  minutes of cumulative intentional pause.
- **Encores:** brighter, closer to conversational. They are the daily
  format — heaviness would make them skippable.
- **Standbys:** eyes-open energy start to finish. Direct, present-tense,
  almost coaching. No trance drawl.
- **The anchor** (installed in every First Sitting, fired in every Encore):
  one breath in + one exhale + one short phrase in your voice. Choose the
  phrase before day A; it must be identical — wording, rhythm, tone —
  in all 36 tracks. It is the product's signature move. Choose something
  you can also say on stage and on camera without it sounding like a
  script. Two-to-four words.
- Multilingual future: these architectures are language-independent. A
  Chinese or French edition is a re-voicing of the same beat sheets with a
  translated anchor phrase — decided per language, then frozen the same way.

**Safety framing (structural, non-negotiable):**

- Walk-ins and the orientation video state: never while driving; Standbys
  are the only tracks designed for eyes open in public.
- Sleep-facing tracks contain **no awakening** — they end downward, into
  the bed, then silence.
- Every other track ends with a **full re-alert**: count-up, energy
  returning, eyes open, explicitly back.

## 4. Beat sheets

### 4.1 Shared architectures

Every track of a type follows its architecture; program-specific content
slots into the marked beats. Design once, voice fresh per program.

**A — The First Sitting (15:00) — appears in all four programs**

| Time | Beat | Must accomplish |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–1:00 | Pre-talk echo | 60-second audio version of the three misconceptions (control, secrets, stuck) — for the buyer who skipped the video. Warm, brief, done. |
| 1:00–4:00 | Induction | Eye fixation or breath-led. Teach it as a *skill they now own*, not something done to them. |
| 4:00–6:00 | Deepener | Progressive; establish the "every breath, deeper" convention reused program-wide. |
| 6:00–7:30 | **Anchor install** | Breath + exhale + the phrase. Fire it three times inside this beat. Tell them exactly what it will do in every Encore. |
| 7:30–12:00 | Program seed | The program's first change-work pass — see per-program rows below. Plant, don't push; this track is heard 3× in days 1–3. |
| 12:00–13:30 | Future pace | One concrete scene of tomorrow with the change present. |
| 13:30–15:00 | Re-alert | Full count-up. Post-hypnotic bridge: "each time you return, faster and deeper." |

Program seed content (beat 7:30–12:00):

| Program | The seed |
|---|---|
| FOMO | The feeling of *already being where you should be*. Presence as the scarce good, not the party. |
| Doomscrolling | The reach noticed in slow motion — one clear frame of choice between urge and act. |
| Pregame-Postgame | The performance state named and felt once: tall, warm, loaded. This is what all pregame tracks will call up. |
| Language | Permission to be a beginner out loud. The accent as a signature, not a flaw. |

**B — Themed Sitting (15:00) — S2/S3 of each program**

| Time | Beat | Must accomplish |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:30 | Arrival | One or two lines, then down to business. Buyers on hearing #3 will thank you. |
| 0:30–3:00 | Re-entry | Fire the anchor; compressed deepener. Faster than S1 — earned speed. |
| 3:00–11:00 | Change work | Three movements: **dismantle** (the old pattern seen from outside) → **install** (the replacement, sensory and specific) → **rehearse** (run the replacement in imagination twice). |
| 11:00–13:00 | Future pace | The replacement placed into a real named situation from their week. |
| 13:00–15:00 | Re-alert | Full count-up. One-line link to the track that comes next in the map. |

**C — The Keeper (15:00) — final Sitting of each program**

Architecture B, with three overrides:

- **Zero calendar language.** No "this week," no day numbers, no references
  to the arc. It is heard in month one and year three.
- More state, less teaching: dismantle beat shrinks, rehearse beat grows.
- The re-alert closes with ownership framing: the skill is theirs, the
  anchor is theirs, this track is the place they come back to.

**D — Encore (4:00–6:00)**

| Time | Beat | Must accomplish |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:30 | Anchor fire | The phrase, once. Drop is assumed, not rebuilt. |
| 0:30–1:00 | Compressed deepener | "You know the way down." |
| 1:00–4:00 | Core suggestion | ONE suggestion from the program's Sittings, restated tight. Morning variants aim at the day ahead; night variants close the day. |
| 4:00–5:00 | Re-alert | Quick, bright, out. |

**D-r — The Restart (6:00) — FOMO and Doomscrolling**

Encore architecture with a mandated emotional shape: **no penance beat.**
Normalize the lapse in one sentence (data, not verdict), fire the anchor,
re-install the core suggestion, future-pace the next ordinary day, out.
The word "again" is banned; the frame is *continuing*, not starting over.

**E — Standby (2:00–4:00) — eyes open, no induction**

| Time | Beat | Must accomplish |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Interrupt | Arrive mid-situation. First line meets them exactly where the trigger is ("Phone's in your hand."). |
| 0:15–1:30 | Redirect | Body first (breath, posture, one physical instruction), then the reframe — one image, not an essay. |
| 1:30–end | Choice point | Hand the decision back, framed. End with them in motion, eyes open, no re-alert needed. |

## 5. Files, names, metadata

**Program codes:** `fomo` · `scroll` · `pregame` · `lang`

**File name schema:** `{program}-{seq}-{type}-{slug}.mp3`
Sequence = room order, two digits. Type ∈ `sitting|encore|standby`.

```
fomo-01-sitting-the-first-sitting.mp3
fomo-02-sitting-enough-ness.mp3
fomo-03-sitting-timeline-trust.mp3
fomo-04-encore-morning.mp3
fomo-05-encore-night.mp3

fomo-07-standby-seeing-the-story.mp3
fomo-08-standby-the-decline.mp3
fomo-09-sitting-the-keeper.mp3
```

(The Keeper is always seq 09 — last in every program.)

**ID3 tags (every file):**

| Tag | Value |
|---|---|
| Artist / Album Artist | The American Hypnotist |
| Album | Program name (e.g. "Stop Doomscrolling") |
| Title | `Part name — Track title` (e.g. "Sitting — Enough-ness", "Standby — Phone in Hand") |

## 6. Video day

13 pieces, one setup, one day. Every piece is direct-address, chest-up,
brand look (midnight ground, gold accent, Fraunces titles on the cards).

**Format:** 4K/24p, lav + boom, brand lighting. **Bullet points, never a
teleprompter** — the entire premise of these videos is that they don't feel
produced. Two takes per piece, pick the warmer one.

**Wardrobe:** one base look for the day; one small variation per program
(jacket/no jacket, color of shirt) so the four programs read as siblings,
not clones. The orientation video uses the base look.

### Shot list

| # | Piece | Length | Must accomplish |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Orientation — "How this works" (shared) | 4:00 | What hypnosis is (30 s) · the three misconceptions: control, secrets, stuck (90 s) · how to sit, when to listen, never while driving (60 s) · how the room works: Today card, Standbys, The Keeper (60 s). **No program names** — evergreen across all four and any future program. |
| 2–5 | Walk-ins ×4 | 3:00 each | Why this program exists (personal, 45 s — Language leads with the five-languages story; Pregame with a stage story) · what's in the room and the two-week shape (60 s) · what to do right now: First Sitting tonight (30 s) · one warm line of welcome. |
| 6–9 | Check-ins ×4 | 1:30 each | Day-7 face. Name what's normal at day 7 (doubt, "is this working?", missed days) · one adjustment (per program: FOMO — notice the feed differently yet?; Scroll — the window is where the work is; Pregame — the rehearsal is the win; Language — messy is the method) · point at Act II / Map 2. |
| 10–13 | Send-offs ×4 | 1:30 each | Mark the finish plainly, no confetti · introduce The Keeper and what it's for (the forever track) · the room is open, everything is theirs · one line that lands like a handshake. |

**Run order for the day:** orientation first (longest, freshest) → four
Walk-ins (highest energy demand) → lunch → four Send-offs (warmth) → four
Check-ins (most conversational, safe for the tired hour).

## 7. Audio-day runsheets

Same shape both days. ~6 hours each including breaks; finished audio per
day is ~2¾ h, so the pace is comfortable, not lazy.

**Day A: FOMO → Stop Doomscrolling. Day B: Pregame-Postgame → Language.**

Per program (~2.5 h):

| Slot | What | Why this order |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Warm-up: read the program's S1 opening aloud twice, off-mic | Voice into the program's register. |
| 2 | S1 The First Sitting | The anchor is born here; everything after references it. |
| 3 | S2, then S3 | Core change work while the voice is strongest. |
| 4 | **Listen-back checkpoint:** 2 min of S1 at speaker volume | Catch mic drift, mouth noise, pace problems before the short tracks inherit them. |
| 5 | S4 The Keeper | Still in deep register from the Sittings. |
| 6 | Encores ×3 | Register shift upward — do all three together. |
| 7 | Standbys ×2 | Eyes-open energy last; it's the easiest to hit when tired. |
| 8 | Pickup list | Re-record any flagged lines while the room is hot. |

Rules of the day:

- The anchor phrase is printed and taped to the mic stand. Same words, same
  rhythm, every take, both days.
- Water, no dairy, no long breaks between S-tracks of one program (register
  drift).
- Every track gets a slate ("fomo oh-two, take one") — trimmed in post.
- If a track fights you twice, skip it, note it, return at slot 8. Never
  grind — grind is audible.

## 8. QC checklist (per track, before "done")

- [ ] Loudness at target (−16 / −18 LUFS per class), true peak ≤ −1.5 dBTP
- [ ] Head/tail room tone trimmed to 0.5 s in, natural tail out
- [ ] Anchor phrase present and identical (word + rhythm) — Sittings & Encores
- [ ] Re-alert present and complete — **except** the three sleep-facing
      tracks, which must have none and must end into silence
- [ ] Standbys contain no eyes-closed instruction
- [ ] Keeper contains zero calendar language (search: "day", "week", "act")
- [ ] No calendar language in any Standby (they're forever-tracks too)
- [ ] Bed level ≤ −28 LUFS, ducked under speech, absent in final minute of
      sleep-facing tracks
- [ ] File named per schema, all ID3 fields set, artwork embedded
- [ ] Played once on phone speaker + once on AirPods, full length, by a
      human

Sign-off is per program, not per track: a program ships when all nine files
pass and the three videos are cut.
