# The Listening Maps

Version 1.0 · 2026-07-08 · companion to PROGRAM-STRUCTURE-SPEC.md v2.0

The Listening Map is Side B of each program's one printed page, and it is
the logic behind the room's Today card. It answers exactly one question per
day: *what do I play, and when?*

These maps are also the filming guide: they fix the order, job, and usage
moment of every track before anything is recorded.

Shared rules (printed at the foot of every map):

1. **Standbys are always in season.** Use them at the moment, every time,
   from day 1. They are not part of the schedule; they are the toolkit.
2. **Miss a day? Shift, never stack.** The map slides forward. Two Sittings
   in one day is not catching up — spacing is the mechanism.
3. **Miss two or more? Play The Restart, then resume where you left off.**
   No restarting from day 1. Restarting from zero is how people quit.
4. **Sittings want eyes closed and 15 undisturbed minutes.** Evening
   beats morning if you have both. Never while driving.

## 2. Stop Doomscrolling — 14 days, trigger-keyed

This map runs on **your window** — the worst scroll hour, read off Screen
Time before day 1 (it's step 1 of the Quick Start). Sittings are scheduled
*against the window*, not at a comfortable hour: the new pattern is
rehearsed where the old one lives.

Track roster:

| Code | Track | Length | Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | The First Sitting | 15:00 | Sitting — state + anchor |
| S2 | Urge-Surfing | 15:00 | Sitting |
| S3 | The Replacement Ritual | 15:00 | Sitting |
| S4 | The Keeper | 15:00 | Sitting — locked until day 14 |
| E1 | Morning Encore | 5:00 | Encore |
| E2 | Night Encore | 5:00 | Encore |
| E3 | The Restart | 6:00 | Encore — lapse reset |
| B1 | Phone in Hand | 2:00 | Standby — at the reach, or mid-scroll |
| B2 | The Put-Down | 3:00 | Standby — bedtime disengage, nightly |

Standing orders, day 1 to forever:

- **B1 at every noticed reach.** Eyes open, phone can stay in your hand.
  A "failed" use (you scrolled anyway) still counts — noticing is the rep.
- **B2 every night** at the moment the phone would follow you to bed.
- **The Environment sheet before day 1.** Five settings changes, ten
  minutes, half the result.

### The map

| Day | Play | When | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | The Environment sheet · find your window | Before starting | The battlefield is chosen before the training. |
| 1 | Walk-in video, then S1 | 15 min before your window | The entrance is learned adjacent to the trigger. |
| 2 | S1 | Before your window | Anchor, second pass. |
| 3 | S1 · rehearse B1 once, calm | Before your window | Tool rehearsed before the emergency. |
| 4 | S2 | Before your window | Urges become something you ride, not obey. |
| 5 | E1 · S2 | Morning · before window | — |
| 6 | S2 | Before your window | — |
| 7 | Check-in video · S1 | Any · before window | Face at the midpoint; re-measure the state. |
| 8 | S3 | Before your window | The hole the scroll leaves needs a tenant. |
| 9 | E1 · S3 | Morning · before window | — |
| 10 | S3 | Before your window | — |
| 11 | E1 · E2 only | Morning · night | The short form takes over. |
| 12 | S2 **or** S3 | Before your window | Your call — ownership rep. |
| 13 | E1 · E2 | Morning · night | — |
| 14 | S3 · Send-off video | Evening | **The Keeper unlocks.** |

**Lapse protocol** (printed on the map, matter-of-fact): a binge night is
data, not a verdict. Play E3 The Restart once, then resume at the day you
left. The map has no shame column.

### Quick Start (Side A)

1. Open Screen Time. Find your worst hour. Write it on this card — that's
   your window.
2. Do The Environment sheet (10 minutes, five settings). Then watch the
   Walk-in and play The First Sitting 15 minutes before your window.
3. From tonight: The Put-Down at bedtime, every night. Phone in Hand at
   every reach you catch.

## 3. Pregame-Postgame — one training week, then event-driven

Two maps: the Training Week (once), then the Event Map (forever). The
program's clock is your calendar of performances, games, talks, dates,
negotiations — "event" means anything with a before and an after.

Track roster:

| Code | Track | Length | Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | The First Sitting | 15:00 | Sitting — state + anchor |
| S2 | The Night Before | 15:00 | Sitting — pregame, sleep-adjacent |
| S3 | The Wind-Down | 15:00 | Sitting — postgame, ends toward sleep |
| S4 | The Keeper | 15:00 | Sitting — locked until first full event cycle |
| E1 | One Hour Out | 6:00 | Encore — pregame |
| E2 | Quick Reset | 4:00 | Encore — between back-to-back events |
| E3 | The Tune-Up | 5:00 | Encore — maintenance on non-event weeks |
| B1 | Final Five | 3:00 | Standby — eyes open, hallway-usable, minutes before |
| B2 | The Debrief | 4:00 | Standby — immediately after, before the phone |

### Map 1 — the Training Week

| Day | Play | When | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Walk-in video, then S1 | Evening | Learn the entrance. |
| 2 | S1 | Evening | Anchor, second pass. |
| 3 | S1 · rehearse B1 once, calm | Evening | Final Five must be boring-familiar before it's ever needed. |
| 4 | S2 | In bed, night | Rehearse the night-before ritual with no event tomorrow. |
| 5 | S3 | Evening | Rehearse the release with nothing to release. |
| 6 | Check-in video · E1 | Any · anytime | Learn the one-hour form cold. |
| 7 | **Dress rehearsal:** E1 → B1 → B2 → S3, spaced across the day | Timed like a real event | The full cycle runs once before it's live. |

### Map 2 — the Event Map (the Run of Show card)

| Moment | Play | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Night before, in bed | S2 The Night Before | Ends toward sleep — no awakening. |
| T − 60 min | E1 One Hour Out | Seated if possible. |
| T − 5 min | B1 Final Five | Eyes open. Standing, hallway, wings. |
| T + 15 min (before checking your phone) | B2 The Debrief | The event ends when you say it ends. |
| That night, in bed | S3 The Wind-Down | Ends toward sleep. |
| Back-to-back events | E2 Quick Reset between | Replaces the Debrief when there's no gap. |
| No event this week | E3 The Tune-Up, twice | The state stays trained between engagements. |

**The Keeper unlocks after your first full real cycle** — one pregame play
and one postgame play on an actual event day. Not a calendar date; a rite.

### Quick Start (Side A)

1. Tonight: Walk-in, then The First Sitting. The training week starts now,
   event or no event on the horizon.
2. Put your next real event's date on this card. Training exists for it.
3. Save Final Five and The Debrief offline now — venues have bad wifi.

## 4. Language Learning — 7-day install, then paired to study

This program attaches to a study habit; it doesn't replace one. Encores are
**bookends** around every study block. The map is honest about mechanism:
these tracks train the learner — fearlessness, attention, consistency — not
the vocabulary.

Track roster:

| Code | Track | Length | Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | The First Sitting | 15:00 | Sitting — state + anchor |
| S2 | Fearless Speaker | 15:00 | Sitting |
| S3 | Open Ear | 15:00 | Sitting — attention/immersion |
| S4 | The Keeper | 15:00 | Sitting — locked until 10 logged bookends |
| E1 | Before Study | 5:00 | Encore — primer, opens every study block |
| E2 | After Study | 6:00 | Encore — consolidation, closes every study block |
| E3 | After Study — Sleep | 15:00 | Encore — the E2 for night studiers; ends toward sleep |
| B1 | Before the Conversation | 3:00 | Standby — the minute before speaking to a human |
| B2 | After the Mistake | 2:00 | Standby — right after the embarrassing error |

### Map 1 — the Install Week

| Day | Play | When | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Walk-in video, then S1 | Evening | Learn the entrance. |
| 2 | S1 | Evening | Anchor, second pass. |
| 3 | S2 | Evening | The identity the whole program serves. |
| 4 | S2 · first paired block: E1 → study | Evening · at study time | Bookending starts while installing. |
| 5 | S3 · E2 after today's study | Evening · after study | Both bookends now live. |
| 6 | Full pair: E1 → study → E2 | At study time | The shape of every week from now on. |
| 7 | Check-in video · S2 **or** S3 | Any · evening | Face at the midpoint; your first choice. |

### Map 2 — the Pairing Protocol (ongoing)

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Every study block gets bookends | E1 before, E2 after. No exceptions smaller than "the bus arrived." |
| Studying within an hour of bed | E3 replaces E2. Ends toward sleep — no awakening. |
| Any live conversation coming | B1 in the minute before. Eyes open. |
| Any cringe moment | B2 immediately — before the story about it hardens. |
| Once a month | One full Sitting of your choice, first Sunday. The long form stays warm. |
| Plateau or missed week | The pairing resumes at the very next study block. The streak is the study habit's job; this program's job is making you show up unafraid. |

**The Keeper unlocks at 10 logged bookend pairs.** Ten pairs ≈ two honest
weeks of study — earned, not waited for.

### Quick Start (Side A)

1. Tonight: Walk-in, then The First Sitting. (The Walk-in is where the
   five-languages story lives — watch it once for real.)
2. Decide your study slot for this week and write it on this card. The
   bookends attach to it starting day 4.
3. Save Before the Conversation offline now. Your next real conversation
   won't schedule itself.

## Room-UI notes (for the Listening Room build)

- The Today card is a direct render of these maps: `program day → track`.
  Pregame-Postgame and Language switch the Today card to their Map 2 logic
  after day 7 ("Next event?" / "Studying today?").
- Day counters advance on practice, not on calendar: a missed day holds the
  map in place (rule 2). Store `last_completed_day`, not a start date.
- Keeper unlock conditions per program: FOMO/Doomscrolling — day 14
  complete; Pregame-Postgame — one S2/E1/B1 play **and** one B2/S3 play
  with `event` flag; Language — 10 E1+E2 (or E3) pairs.
- Every map's four shared rules render in the room footer, verbatim.
